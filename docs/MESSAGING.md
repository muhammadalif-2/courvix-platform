# Courvix messaging and conversion plan

## Positioning

Courvix is the operational home for the people running sports clubs and personal training businesses. Two products connect the people, schedules and payment records that otherwise live in separate chats and spreadsheets.

Primary audiences: club/academy owners managing groups and recurring dues; independent trainers/studios managing paid sessions and client progress. The primary buyer needs control and less follow-up. Staff need a clear next action. Members and clients need confidence about their next session and payment status.

## Message hierarchy (written before the redesign)

1. **Hero:** More coaching. Less chasing.
2. **Explanation:** Bring your people, schedules and payment records into one clear workspace. Choose Club Management or Personal Training, built around the way your business runs.
3. **Problem:** The session is simple. Everything around it gets complicated. Separate rosters, chat threads and payment notes make it hard to know who is coming, what is paid and what needs attention.
4. **Product choice:** Run a club that stays in sync. / Build a training business around every client.
5. **Mechanism:** Invite the right people. Give every role a clear view. Keep the next session and next payment visible.
6. **Proof:** A working, explicitly labelled sample workspace. Show the actual journey and role boundaries rather than invented testimonials, adoption numbers or time-saving claims.
7. **Objections:** Explain ChatGPT sign-in, manually verified bank transfers, per-location subscriptions, trial/expiry, email configuration and export access.
8. **Action:** Explore the demo (primary before account commitment), Start a 14-day trial (secondary), Choose your product, Talk about your setup.

## Product messages

**Club Management:** Members, teams, coaches, attendance and recurring dues. See what needs attention without rebuilding the story from different spreadsheets. Workflow: organize → invite → schedule → record → follow up.

**Personal Training:** Sell one visit or a session pack. Verify payment, reserve a slot and record progress, with a session balance your client can understand. Workflow: offer → verify → book → coach → review.

## Voice and constraints

Plain English. Concrete verbs and outcomes. Short headlines supported by specific product behavior. Avoid pressure countdowns, fake scarcity, fake reviews, guaranteed growth, medical outcomes, unsupported savings and claims that payment/email services are active before they are configured.

Prices stay IDR 250,000 / 500,000 per workspace per 30-day paid term, with a 14-day initial trial. No credit-card autocharge; training purchases and club dues go to each business. Pro adds scheduled setup/onboarding support, with scope agreed before purchase. Every location needs its own subscription. Email invitations/reminders require a verified sender and enabled scheduling. Sign-in currently uses a ChatGPT account matching the invitation email.

## Page goals

- Home: understand the problem, see the product, choose a route.
- Products / Club / Personal Training: recognize the correct workflow and try its demo.
- How it works: understand ownership, roles, locations and payment boundaries.
- Pricing: understand the paid unit, term, support difference and payment method.
- Sports: establish operational fit without implying every sport's scoring rules exist.
- Contact / custom work: open a real email draft; never simulate a successful enquiry.
- Sign-in / invitation / registration: explain why an account is needed and preserve selected product.
- Application: consistent navigation, next actions, readable tables, clear payment states and honest service readiness.
